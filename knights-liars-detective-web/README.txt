Knights & Liars Detective - browser version
-------------------------------------------
Just double-click index.html. It runs entirely in your browser: no install, no internet.

Optional (to serve it at http://localhost:8000): run start-server.bat (Windows) or
start-server.sh (Mac/Linux). Needs Python. Not required - index.html works on its own.

Files: index.html (page), style.css, logic.js (expression trees, solver, puzzle generator),
app.js (interface).
